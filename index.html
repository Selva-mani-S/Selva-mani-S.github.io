Selected work

Municipal WaterTax ERP

Client: NICSI / Government of Puducherry. Built at AeoLogic Technologies, Nov 2024 to present.

Problem
Water billing, connection approvals and counter collections for 221K+ consumers needed one reliable system, with online payment and tight security.
What I built
  • Billing engine with tariff slabs, arrears and surcharges, plus new-connection approval and counter-collection modules, on Ardalis Clean Architecture and FastEndpoints.
  • BBPS, GRAS and LGRAMS payment integrations, with idempotent Coravel jobs that reconcile dropped transactions.
  • FIDO2 passkeys, OTP-based 2FA, and SQL-backed sessions that allow one active session per user.
  • SignalR cashier notifications and QuestPDF demand notices, receipts and bills.
Result
Database storage down 70% by streaming attachments to self-hosted MinIO with ZIP compression. Upgraded the platform from .NET 8 to .NET 10 and replaced MediatR with a source-generated Mediator after MediatR became commercial.

Employment Exchange Platform

Client: NICSI / Labour Department, Puducherry. Built at SISL Infotech, May 2023 to Oct 2024.

Problem
Four regional exchanges served 472K+ job seekers on a legacy .NET Core 3.1 codebase with costly, memory-heavy reporting.
What I built
  • Online registration, 3-year renewal, regional transfer and vacancy sponsorship on ASP.NET Core MVC and PostgreSQL.
  • A 430+ entity EF Core model with composite keys (Exchange, Regyear, Regno).
  • Session-bound salt challenge-response login and middleware that enforces a single active session.
  • Sync with the National Career Service portal and the NIC SMS Gateway over WCF/SOAP and REST.
Result
Moved the platform from .NET Core 3.1 to .NET 8. Replaced DevExpress with QuestPDF and ClosedXML for 15+ statutory reports, removing license costs and cutting PDF memory use by about 40%. Reports now stream into a PDF.js viewer without temporary files on the server.
